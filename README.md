AVIONICS SUITE
____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
UPDATE

v1.2 Avionics Suite was created thanks to your support and feedback:

- EFB management
- GSX management
- SimBrief flight plan upload
- Notepad using keyboard, finger, or stylus

V1.1 Added support for GSX Pro; you can now log in to both services and quickly switch between ground services and the EFB via a pop-out menu.

_____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
Manage your EFB and GSX Pro in a single app.

You can also import your flight plan data to access useful information for departures and arrivals, and jot down notes using your finger, a stylus, or the keyboard.
